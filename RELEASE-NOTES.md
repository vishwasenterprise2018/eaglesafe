# EAGLE SAFE v6

Final release based on the approved v6.2 demo. Website code, product data and images are unchanged from that demo.

## Included

- Liquid Glass Day Mode and blue Liquid Glass Night Mode across the homepage, catalogue and product pages.
- All 55 product models, including MSMT10, MSMT11 and LSMT18. No AR feature.
- Larger centred EAGLE SAFE homepage title without a subtitle.
- Full-width slideshow images within their frames, without white side strips.
- Original gold, jewellery, cash and documents introduction and its actions below the slideshow.
- Alternating desktop collection layouts, theme-aware product-image surrounds and a compact footer.
- Two-column mobile catalogue, mobile filter drawer and aligned range-control labels.
- Saved theme applied before page content loads to prevent a Day Mode flash during Night Mode navigation.
- Existing enquiry form connection, contact details, brochures and product specifications retained.

## Verification

The source demo passed homepage checks at 1920, 1440, 768, 390 and 320 pixels in both themes. Catalogue filtering, filter persistence, product galleries, unit conversion and image zoom were also checked in desktop and mobile browser layouts. Live enquiry submission was not performed.

## Upload

Upload the contents of this release folder to the GitHub Pages publishing root, including assets, images, product_pages, CNAME and .nojekyll. Keep the existing folder structure and filename case. This release does not publish automatically.

The build_v5.mjs generator and apply_v6.mjs integration script are included for future maintenance. No build is required to view or upload the website.
